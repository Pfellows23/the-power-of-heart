The Hearts of Mauna Kea

On the Kohala Coast of Hawai‘i Island stands the Mauna Kea Beach Hotel, the first of Laurance Rockefeller’s resorts, opened in 1965 on the white crescent of Kauna‘oa Bay and built to honor the land and sea around it.

I arrived there in a season of my life when everything was shifting. What followed was a quiet run of serendipity: chance meetings, perfect timing, and heart-shaped stones appearing on the shore as if they had been waiting for me.

This book gathers those moments. It is a record of how a rare place, the right time, and the right people can set the unexpected in motion and awaken the soul.
