Why the heart

The oldest intelligence we have

Long before anyone studied the mind, the great wisdom traditions pointed to the heart as the seat of knowing. Most of us were never taught how to use it.

This is not sentiment. The heart is a center of intelligence, where intuition, courage, and truth meet. The mind can analyze a life, but only the heart can tell you whether it is yours. When it leads, decisions become clear, relationships become honest, and purpose stops being something to chase and becomes something to live.

Mindfulness taught a generation to quiet the mind. Heartfulness is the next step, and it has never mattered more. In a world that is louder, faster, and more divided than ever, a quiet mind is no longer enough. The awareness these traditions have guarded all along is still waiting to be discovered.
